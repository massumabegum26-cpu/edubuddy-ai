Quantum Superposition

The Core Idea (TL;DR)

While classical computers use bits that must be strictly 0 or 1, quantum computers use qubits that can explore both simultaneously until observed.

Intuitive Metaphor / Analogy

Think of a coin spinning on a table. While it's spinning in mid-air, it is neither purely Heads nor Tails—it holds the mathematical possibility of both. Classical bits are coins sitting flat on the table; qubits are coins spinning in motion.

Common Student Misconception

Students often think quantum computers just run regular games or spreadsheets 1,000x faster. In reality, they are completely different mathematical engines designed specifically for combinatorics like molecule simulations.
